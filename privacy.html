Privacy Policy

CODEHUB uses Supabase Auth for account sign-in and a database to store display names and quiz scores. Do not enter sensitive personal information.

Quiz scores and display names may appear on the public leaderboard. Account email addresses are not shown on the leaderboard. Contact the site owner to request account/data assistance.

This is a starter policy for a demo. Before public commercial launch, update it to describe all actual data collection, analytics, advertising cookies, retention and contact details, and obtain appropriate legal advice.
